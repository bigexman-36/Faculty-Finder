How to use: 1) Set your start: Use my location, Try demo location (pretend you're near Technology), or Pick start on map then tap the map. 2) Search for your destination above, or pick it from the map or the list. 3) Tap Preview walking route. Faded pins are approximate and not yet verified.

Routes and times are estimates. Follow paths and signs, and stay aware of your surroundings. Privacy: your location stays in your browser, except that route requests send your start and destination points to a public OpenStreetMap routing service. Pin data comes from a community survey and hasn't been checked on the ground yet; if a pin looks wrong, tap "Report a wrong pin" on its card.
